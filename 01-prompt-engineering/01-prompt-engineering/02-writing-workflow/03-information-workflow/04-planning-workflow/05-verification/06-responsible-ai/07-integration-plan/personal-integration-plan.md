# Personal Integration Plan

## Purpose

This plan identifies recurring workplace tasks where AI can support project coordination while keeping human review and accountability.

## AI Integration Plan

| Recurring Workplace Task           | Expected Benefit                                                       | Preferred Prompt / Workflow                                                                                      | Risk & Verification                                                                      | Frequency               | Success Measure                                      |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Project Status Updates             | Save time when organizing project information into a clear update      | Use the Information Processing Workflow: Raw Input → Prompt → AI Output → Human Review → Final Structured Output | AI may misinterpret project information; verify all facts against the original source    | Weekly                  | Faster preparation with accurate final updates       |
| Meeting Notes and Action Items     | Convert notes into a structured list of actions and owners             | Use a structured prompt to extract decisions, actions, owners, and due dates from provided notes                 | AI may assign or infer information incorrectly; verify each action and owner             | After relevant meetings | Clearer action tracking and less manual organization |
| Professional Project Communication | Improve clarity and consistency when drafting project-related messages | Use the Writing Workflow: Draft → Verify → Refine → Human Sign-off                                               | AI may change the intended meaning or add unsupported information; review before sending | As needed               | Clear, concise, and accurate communication           |

## Human Review

AI-generated outputs should be reviewed before being used in workplace activities. The Project Coordinator remains responsible for confirming accuracy, protecting confidential information, and approving the final output.

## Success Criteria

AI integration will be considered useful when it:

* Reduces time spent on repetitive organization and drafting tasks.
* Improves the structure and clarity of workplace outputs.
* Does not introduce unsupported information.
* Allows the Project Coordinator to maintain control over the final result.
* Supports work without replacing human judgment.
