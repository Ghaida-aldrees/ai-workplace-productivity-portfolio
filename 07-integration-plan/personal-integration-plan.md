# Personal Integration Plan

## Purpose

This plan identifies recurring workplace tasks where AI could support productivity while keeping human review and responsibility in place.

## AI Integration Opportunities

| Recurring Workplace Task | Expected Benefit | Preferred AI Workflow | Risk / Verification | Frequency | Success Indicator |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Preparing project status updates | Faster and more consistent reporting | Provide project information → structure the update → review and refine | Verify all information against the source and remove unsupported details | Weekly | Clear and timely status updates |
| Organizing project information | Faster conversion of raw information into structured outputs | Provide source information → categorize key details → human review | Check accuracy, missing information, dates, and project status | As needed | Less manual effort to structure information |
| Preparing project plans and follow-up activities | Better organization of tasks and next steps | Define goal → generate phases and tasks → review and adjust | Confirm that tasks, responsibilities, and timelines are realistic and supported | As needed | Clearer action plans and easier follow-up |

## Responsible Use

AI should be used as a productivity support tool rather than a replacement for human judgment.

Before using an AI-generated output:
- Verify important information.
- Do not provide confidential or personal information unless authorized.
- Check for unsupported assumptions or fabricated details.
- Review the final output before sharing it.
- Follow organizational AI and information-security policies.

## Key Learning

The most useful AI workflows are those that support recurring tasks while keeping the human responsible for verification, decisions, and final approval.
