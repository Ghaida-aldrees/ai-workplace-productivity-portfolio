# Responsible AI Use Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist supports responsible use of AI in workplace tasks. AI outputs should be reviewed by a human before they are used or shared.

## Six Responsible AI Concepts

| Concept | Workplace Consideration |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Check AI-generated information against reliable source material. |
| Accountability | The person using AI remains responsible for the final output. |
| Confidentiality | Do not provide confidential company information to AI tools unless approved. |
| Privacy | Avoid entering personal or sensitive information into AI tools. |
| Bias | Review outputs for unfair assumptions, missing perspectives, or biased language. |
| Human Oversight | A human should review important AI-generated outputs before use. |

## Information Classification

| Information Type | Classification | Appropriate Use |
|---|---|---|
| Public workplace information | Green | Can generally be used with AI, subject to company policy. |
| Internal non-sensitive information | Amber | Use only when permitted by organizational policy and with appropriate caution. |
| Confidential or personal information | Red | Do not enter into AI tools unless explicitly authorized and protected by approved controls. |

## Practical Checks Before Using AI Output

- Confirm that the information is accurate.
- Remove or correct unsupported assumptions.
- Check that confidential or personal information has not been exposed.
- Review the output for bias or inappropriate wording.
- Confirm that the final decision remains with a human.
- Follow the organization's AI and information-security policies.

## Key Principle

AI can support workplace productivity, but responsible use requires accuracy checks, protection of information, and human oversight.

**No real confidential, private, or sensitive organizational data should be used in this portfolio.**
