## 🎯 Project Overview

This portfolio demonstrates how generative AI can support a **Project Coordinator** in completing recurring workplace tasks more efficiently.

It includes practical applications in prompt engineering, professional writing, information processing, planning, verification, and responsible AI use.

All examples are designed around a professional project coordination scenario. AI is used as a productivity assistant, while accuracy, judgment, verification, and final approval remain under human responsibility.

## 💡 Key Learnings

1. Clear prompts with context, requirements, and expected outputs produce more useful and consistent results.
2. AI-generated information should be checked against the original source before use.
3. Missing owners, dates, or other information should be marked as `[Not Specified]` rather than invented.
4. Privacy and confidentiality should be considered before using workplace information with AI tools.
5. AI can support productivity and organization, but human judgment and final responsibility remain essential.
6. Reusable AI workflows can make recurring project coordination tasks more structured and efficient.

---

## 🛡️ Responsible Use Statement

This portfolio does not contain real confidential, private, personal, or sensitive organizational information.

AI is used as a productivity support tool and not as a replacement for human judgment or decision-making.

AI-assisted outputs should be reviewed by a human before use or sharing. Information should be checked for accuracy, completeness, unsupported assumptions, bias, and appropriate tone.

Confidential, personal, or sensitive information should not be entered into AI tools unless explicitly authorized and protected by approved controls.

When information is unavailable, `[Not Specified]` is used instead of making assumptions.
